Chip Room self-hosts the following SIL Open Font License 1.1 webfonts.

body.woff2 — Noto Sans SC, variable Chinese interface subset
Upstream: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/notosanssc
License: body-OFL.txt

display.woff2 — Noto Serif SC, variable heading and numeral subset
Upstream: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/notoserifsc
License: display-OFL.txt

figures.woff2 — Barlow Semi Condensed SemiBold, Latin and numeric subset
Upstream: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/barlowsemicondensed
License: figures-OFL.txt

The bundled WOFF2 files are text subsets supplied by the Google Fonts CSS2 API.
Player names may also use the fonts installed on the player's device.
